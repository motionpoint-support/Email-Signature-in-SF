Your Email Signature in Salesforce

Where to set your email signature in Salesforce, the team signature to copy, and how to check it on a case. About 6 minutes, with background music.

Your Salesforce email signature

Type your full name, as it shows in Salesforce and Teams. It replaces [ Full Name ] below. Then copy the signature.

  1. In Salesforce, click your avatar (top right), then Settings.
  2. Left menu: Email › My Email Settings.
  3. Paste it in the Email Signature box. Keep Salesforce Email Composer selected.
  4. Click Save. Then check it: case › Feed › Email › Compose.
